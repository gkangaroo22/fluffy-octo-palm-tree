Birthday Dinner

Midtown / Lincoln Center area
Post-Ballet Birthday Celebration
Exquisite solo dining — you deserve something special!

Top Picks:

· Tatiana by Kwame Onwuachi — 10 Lincoln Center Plaza
Afro-Caribbean fine dining from James Beard Award winner Kwame Onwuachi. Right on the Lincoln Center campus — stunning space, incredible flavors, and a celebration-worthy experience. $100-180

· Florentin — 1568 Broadway (Times Square area)
Modern Israeli/Mediterranean cuisine. Vibrant atmosphere, beautiful plates, and bold flavors. Perfect for a solo birthday celebration at the bar. $80-140

· Osteria La Baia — 38 W 58th St
Elegant Italian in Midtown. Handmade pastas, fresh seafood, and a sophisticated ambiance. Bar seating welcomes solo guests warmly. $90-160

All three are accessible from Lincoln Center — choose based on your mood after the ballet!
Solo dining tip: Sit at the bar — you'll get great service, can chat with the bartender, and it's the most comfortable spot for a solo birthday celebration. Don't be shy about telling them it's your birthday!
Lincoln Center → Restaurants:
Tatiana is right on the Lincoln Center campus (2 min walk). Osteria La Baia is a short walk or quick cab south to 58th St. Florentin is a 10 min walk to Times Square area.
