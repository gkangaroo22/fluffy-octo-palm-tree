Birthday Dinner

Near Lincoln Center, Upper West Side
Post-Ballet Birthday Celebration
Exquisite solo dining near Lincoln Center. You deserve something special!

Top Recommendations:

· Bar Boulud — 1900 Broadway (right across from Lincoln Center)
Chef Daniel Boulud's French bistro & wine bar. Incredible charcuterie, terrines, and wine list. Bar seating is perfect for solo dining. $80-150

· Lincoln Ristorante — 142 W 65th St (on the Lincoln Center campus)
Elegant Italian fine dining with gorgeous interior. Bar seating welcomes solo guests. $100-180

· Café Luxembourg — 200 W 70th St
Classic Upper West Side French bistro, warm and inviting. Comfortable for solo diners, beloved by locals for decades. $80-130

All three are walking distance from Lincoln Center, solo-friendly, and exquisite — choose based on your mood after the ballet!
Solo dining tip: Sit at the bar — you'll get great service, can chat with the bartender, and it's the most comfortable spot for a solo birthday celebration. Don't be shy about telling them it's your birthday!
Lincoln Center → Restaurants:
All three options are within a 5-minute walk of Lincoln Center. Bar Boulud is literally across the street. Lincoln Ristorante is on campus. Café Luxembourg is a short walk north on Amsterdam Ave.
